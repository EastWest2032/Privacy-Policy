Trap Grid Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Summary: Trap Grid does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

This policy explains how the Trap Grid Android app ("the app"), published by EastWest2032, handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send any information off your device. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other third-party tracking software, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, accounts, or device identifiers.

Information stored on your device

To work as a game, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your settings: sound, vibration, safe first tap, question marks, theme, and chosen difficulty.
  • Your game statistics: games played, games won, best times, and win streaks for each difficulty.

This information does not identify you and is never sent to us. You can reset statistics from the Statistics screen in the app, or remove all app data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android backup for your Google account, Android may include the settings and statistics above in your device backup so they can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Permissions

The app requests only the vibrate permission, used for optional haptic feedback during play. You can turn vibration off in the app's settings.

Third parties

The app does not share information with any third party. The app is distributed through Google Play, and Google may collect information about installs and app performance under its own privacy policy, independently of the app.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact
